## 6. Against Ben's brief, and the five L10.4 moves

**Does it read as gothic retro-futurism, between life and death?**

| Half | Reads? | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Gothic | ✅ Yes | Home doors (arch with double rule, death/life symbol pair, fence divider, starfield, IM Fell), the carved story card, crepe and mauve everywhere |
| Retro-futurist | ❌ Not yet | Only the ringed moon (which reads as a planet), Michroma nav labels and corner ticks on two cards. `.instrument`, `.readout` and `.label` are used on **no** screen (`git grep` at d531321) |
| Between life and death | Only on the two home doors | The life/death pairing (mauve vs lichen) appears nowhere inside |

The tool pages are dark recolors of the old layouts: stacked rounded cards and large empty areas below them (Stamp Kit, EVP, `/near` without coordinates).

**Highest-impact L10.4 moves**, ordered by demo-video seconds and by how strongly each says "the brief":

1. **Spirit box and séance as one instrument panel.** This is the video's "haunted" moment.
   - Wrap the box in `.instrument.bezel`.
   - A wisp readout strip in Michroma: `SWEEP 150 MS · VOICES 12/60 · 03:12`.
   - The spoken word stays big in IM Fell bone, with a faint wisp text-shadow; that is the life/death meeting.
   - A CSS-only band needle that sweeps with the step rate (transform only, off for reduced motion).
   - Restyle the sliders as dials with 28 px thumbs.
   - Show the caption log as a ticker inside the panel, not a separate section.
   - Keep "Turn it off near mourners." and the text-to-speech line inside the panel.
2. **Story card as headstone plus plaque.** This card has the most screen time.
   - Keep the arch and add `.arch-rule`.
   - Put one death/life glyph pair at the apex (moon and lily; hourglass and sprout for "Gone 100+ years").
   - Make "STOP 01 / 80" a Michroma label; it is redundant with the heading, so legibility is safe.
   - Set the dates as one inscription line with abbreviated months (this also fixes N8).
   - Plain cards stay untouched (F26 test).
3. **Bingo and party tiles as carved stones that "light up".** The party card is on two phones in the video (L6.2).
   - Each tile is a small arch-top stone with grain, carrying its line-art symbol: lily, poppy, hourglass, candle, moth and key already exist; add willow, urn, lamb, broken column and anchor.
   - A found tile gets the wisp phosphor ring rather than a mauve fill.
4. **Stamp Kit as a darkroom instrument.** Bezel corner ticks around the framing view; Michroma readouts for mode, grit and stroke; the three outputs as specimen cards (perforated edge for the postage stamp). This fills the 700 px void.
5. **Calendar and Gravitator alignment.**
   - The calendar's "Coming up" becomes an almanac: Michroma date readouts (`FRI · NOV 27`), IM Fell names, a lichen sprout for birthdays.
   - Redraw the Gravitator's filled-silhouette crescent moon as the ringed-moon line art, with the starfield, so the app has one illustration language.
