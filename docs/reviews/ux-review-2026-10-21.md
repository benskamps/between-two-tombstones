**Severity:** P0 = blocks the stranger pass or a gate · P1 = fix before freeze (Fri 16:30 UTC) · P2 = nice to have.

## 1. Verdict

1. **The direction is sound.**
   - The dark Gravitator palette reads well (`shots/dark-gravitator-deck2.png`), and text contrast is tested in code (`lib/palette.test.ts`).
   - Decorative SVG is `aria-hidden`.
   - The haunt store is careful: the server render is "unknown", shared links always open, and it falls back to memory when storage is blocked.
   - axe finds nothing above "minor" on prod.
2. **The biggest risks are structural, not visual:**
   - The new hub pushes the stories about 1.3 screens down.
   - Nearest-first lists can bury the one cemetery that has stories.
   - Gating breaks about 20 e2e navigations and changes what Lighthouse measures. All three hit Gate 2 or Gate 11.
3. **Dark-only in daylight is survivable** if every control gets the ash edge and nothing a stranger must read is muted text or a 12px Michroma label.
   - Run today's stranger pass on current prod, or re-time Gate 2 on the preview first. Don't merge Lane 10 between the two.

## 3. Nits (one line each)

**Global and shell**
- Prod: about 200px of blank space before the footer (`main pb-28` plus footer `pb-24`). Keep one.
- Prod: the tagline and the Dark/Daylight toggle are still live. Lane 10 removed both ✓.
- Lane 10: a long haunt name in the header truncates ("All Saints Episcopal Church Cem…"). Fine; keep the full name in `title`.

**Home**
- Lane 10: `HauntReturn` sits after the doors (`app/page.tsx:16`). Put it above them when the haunt is fresh (F10).
- Lane 10: "Uses your location" is a muted 14px hint. Set it in bone, since it is the consent line.
- Lane 10: the placeholder "Cemetery name" plus the eyebrow is the only visible label. Add a visible Atkinson label, "Or type the cemetery's name".
- Lane 10: the "Search" ghost button is invisible in sun (F7). Use an ash edge, or make it mauve-outlined.
- Prod: the etiquette modal autofocuses "Got it" with a blue ring in light mode. Lane 10's wisp ring is fine.

**/near**
- Without coordinates it shows developer copy: "We need a location as lat (−90 to 90) and lon (−180 to 180)". Use "Tap below to use your location, or search by name."
- "No burials listed on Wikidata yet" rows look like every other card and open an empty tour. Make them non-links, or fold them away (F2).

**/search**
- "oakland" returns 2 results (F18). The line "Same names are common, so check the town." is good; keep it.

**/cemetery (hub)**
- "Atlanta · cemetery in Atlanta, Georgia, USA" is redundant. Drop the description when it repeats the place.
- "The 80 best-known people" disagrees with "115 people listed" on the lists. Use "80 of the 115 people listed on Wikidata".
- "2 of 3 so far" on a fresh phone implies the user pinned two graves. Use "2 graves have known locations; pin 1 more with 'I found it'".
- "aged 52" wraps onto its own line. Make "aged N" `whitespace-nowrap`.
- The stop 2 and stop 12 text artifacts (F19), and the ID-as-name case (F20).
- Lane 10: each of the 7 toolkit tiles carries a 2-line description. Trim to icon and title (F1).
- Lane 10: "Tools for X. The tour is below." will be unnecessary once the stories come first.

**/stamp**
- "1. Take or choose a photo" puts an IM Fell numeral in the step heading (F9).
- Lane 10: the gate copy is inaccurate (F12).

**/spirit**
- About 200px of empty space under the disclaimer before Start. Fill it with a standby readout in the instrument panel.
- The range thumbs are small for gloves. Style `::-webkit-slider-thumb` and `::-moz-range-thumb` at 28px.
- axe (minor): `<ol role="log">` is not allowed. Wrap the list in a `div role="log"`.

**/spirit séance**
- After starting, "Start a different séance" is the filled primary while "Open the séance on this phone" is secondary. Swap them, and add a Web Share "Send the link" where available.
- A bad link shows the whole paragraph in bold red. Make one bold sentence and the rest regular; that link is also a dead end in Lane 10 (F13).

**/games**
- Lane 10: Bingo and Party are gated although they need no cemetery (F12).

**/calendar**
- Three filled primary buttons are stacked (Apple, Google, Outlook). Make one primary for the detected platform and the rest outline.

**/gravitator**
- Prod: the green-black header clashes with the crepe-black page. Lane 10 fixes this ✓.
- "Use my location" asks again inside the "Plan a date" door. Fine, because the permission persists.

**Failures**
- "No such place on Wikidata" is good copy.
- The offline first tap gives a blank page (F16).
- The location-denied message is good. After a denial, move focus to the name field.

  - Recent or unknown deaths get the plain card, as with Maynard Jackson, d. 2003 (`shots/dark-cemetery-plain-card.png`).

- **The Gravitator as-is:**
  - Pass, Go now and Save.

  - Sparse cards that still look intentional.
  - The reduced-motion fade.
- **From Lane 10:**
  - The `palette.test.ts` text and boundary pairs.
